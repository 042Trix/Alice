# Governance

## Current model

Alice is intentionally lightweight and is currently governed by a solo-maintainer model. Decision rights rest with the operator, Matthew.

There is no multi-seat governance yet: no voting body, no council, and no standing governance committee. All merge authority and release authority belong to the operator. Contributors and reviewers may provide discussion and recommendations, but participation does not grant merge, release, moderation, or governance authority.

## Decisions

The operator decides project scope, methodology acceptance, contribution disposition, publication, releases, security response, and interpretation of this document and the Code of Conduct. Proposals should explain the problem, alternatives, trade-offs, and impact on method-not-instance discipline.

The operator may request revisions, redirect instance-specific material to `worked-examples/`, merge, close, defer, or reject a proposal. Urgent security or conduct matters may be handled privately before public discussion.

## Future evolution

This is an intentionally lightweight stub and will be revised if the project develops a sustained multi-maintainer community. Any future seats, voting, council, delegated authority, or formal conflict-resolution process will be documented here before being relied upon.
